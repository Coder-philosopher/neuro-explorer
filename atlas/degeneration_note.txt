DEGENERATION BREAK-FIX (2026-10-26)
===================================

SYMPTOM (user report)
  3-dial stack + "zero day" prompt: answer B collapsed into the
  "The example used in the next section is the following: **19.** **20.**"
  repetition spiral, burned 23 s to the token cap; ALSO the UI strip
  claimed "DIAL-led B: none applied" while B clearly ran dials.

ROOT CAUSES
  1. No anti-loop control: engines could emit degenerate loops freely.
  2. Strip showed engine-APPLIED state, not what the user had just sent
     (two different states in the UI).

FIXES (all live)
  a. repeat_penalty param on /api/chat + /api/compare (default 1.10,
     UI input "REPEAT PEN 1.10" in the status strip; 1.0 = off).
  b. server-side degeneration guard (_loop_cut): detects a 40-120 char
     chunk repeating 3x and truncates at its second occurrence with an
     honest "[repetition loop detected]" marker; response carries
     degenerated:true; pane footer shows (LOOP TRUNCATED).
  c. strip now bound to lastSent (send-time truth) and updates instantly.

VERIFIED

  normal prose. Full re-run of the failing scenario (evil1.5+hacker2.0+
  scientific1.0 stack, "zero day" prompt): NO loop, B coherent with
  deformations (junk link) but readable; A normal.

  that spiralled before now degrade gently instead of collapsing.
